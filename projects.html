Titanic

A handcrafted stop-motion animation retelling key moments from the 1997 film *Titanic*. All characters were sculpted from modeling clay and animated frame by frame. The project required planning, scene composition, animation, cinematography, and post-production editing, showcasing both artistic and technical skills in visual storytelling.
